## Brand & Style

This design system defines an artisan-meets-tech aesthetic for premium home improvement and trade services. It avoids the chaotic, cluttered visual tropes of classic classifieds and contractor directories. Instead, it frames craftsmanship, electrical precision, plumbing, and domestic installations with architectural discipline and contemporary digital clarity.

### Personality & Tone
- **Dependable & Authoritative:** Structured, unyielding layouts anchored in deep slate and crisp geometry inspire immediate trust and institutional reliability.
- **Mastery & Warmth:** Energetic amber and warm craftsman tones inject responsiveness, skill, and human touch into structural surfaces.
- **Transparent & Exact:** High legibility, crisp card divisions, clear pricing tiers, and certified credential badges replace opaque contractor ambiguity.

### Aesthetic Methodology
The visual language fuses **Modern Corporate clarity** with **subtle technical tactile accents**. Interfaces feature crystalline container borders, hairline dividing rules, dark-foundation accents, high-contrast review indicators, and luminous amber micro-interactions that signify responsiveness and immediate dispatch across the Vallès Occidental region.

## Colors

The palette balances deep architectural anchor tones with radiant craftsman amber accents to signal both solidity and active responsiveness.

### Roles & Semantic Application
- **Primary (`#0f172a` / `#1e293b`):** The structural core. Used for display typography, authoritative buttons, navigational surfaces, and contrast anchoring. Communicates durability, electrical precision, and permanence.
- **Secondary (`#d97706` / `#f59e0b`):** Craftsman Amber. Reserved for primary conversion calls to action (e.g., "Pedir Presupuesto", "Urgencias 24h"), service status badges, guarantee stars, and high-priority indicators. Evokes warmth, hand tools, and prompt daytime response.
- **Tertiary (`#0284c7`):** Hydro Azure. Used selectively for plumbing diagnostics, certified guarantee seals, verified license flags, and water/climate service indicators.
- **Neutrals (`#f8fafc` canvas, `#f1f5f9` card surface, `#e2e8f0` borders, `#64748b` body copy):** Cool, clinical slate-whites eliminate visual friction, keeping quotes, lists of tasks, and technician profiles airy and legible.
- **Functional States:** Error/Alert (`#dc2626`), Success/Verified (`#16a34a`), Info/Pending (`#0284c7`).

## Typography

The type system relies exclusively on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels, harnessing its geometric poise, humanist warmth, and razor-sharp rendering on mobile screens.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Display & Section Titles:** Set in bold and extrabold weights with negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`). This delivers confidence and an engineering-grade tone without feeling harsh.
- **Body Text:** Scaled for effortless reading on bright outdoors mobile screens where homeowners frequently submit emergency or booking requests. Generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) ensure technical service breakdowns and guarantee terms remain legible.
- **Labels, Badges & Microcopy:** Rendered with medium-to-bold weights and slight positive tracking (`+0.01em` to `+0.04em`) to establish crisp contrast on verification badges, localized city tags (e.g., "Sabadell Centro", "Sant Cugat"), and pricing units.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a rigorous **12-column responsive fluid grid** on desktop, converting to **6 columns** on tablet, and **4 columns** on mobile devices.

### Viewport Adaptation & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Outer margin collapses to `1.25rem` (`margin-mobile`), element spacing defaults to `space-md` (`1rem`), and complex service grids collapse into vertical accordion flows or swipeable horizontal cards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** Margins expand to `2rem`, gutters lock to `1.25rem`. Diagnostic checklists and quote estimate builders shift to 2-column balancing.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Outer margins scale to `3rem` (`margin`), maximum container width caps at `1280px` to maintain optimal line lengths. Multi-step estimate builders and service index matrices span 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters.

### Rhythm Principles
Internal component padding uses strict multiples of `0.25rem` (4px baseline). Cards maintain a consistent internal breathing room using `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) on desktop and `space-md` (`1rem`) on small viewports to guarantee clean separation of service cost specs, icons, and CTAs.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through a modern combination of **low-contrast architectural outlines**, **subtle slate tinted shadows**, and **ambient amber edge halos**. The interface avoids heavy, mudded drop shadows in favor of a crisp physical-mechanical sheet hierarchy.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** Background tone `#f8fafc`. Completely neutral and quiet.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Tiers):** `#ffffff` fill accompanied by a hairline outline (`1px solid #e2e8f0`). Ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Widgets & Hover States):** Applied when hovering over service cards (e.g., "Fontanería", "Electricidad") and active booking drawers. Elevation changes to: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`, with the border transitioning to `#cbd5e1`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals, Overlays & Sticky Booking Bars):** High-priority panels feature crisp backdrop-blur panels (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`) paired with `0 20px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`.
- **Craftsman Glow (Accent Indicator):** For emergency 24h cards or prominent quote buttons, a targeted glow is applied using amber diffusion: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.25), 0 4px 14px 0 rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape standard. This introduces measured, balanced corner geometry that feels contemporary, friendly, and approachable while maintaining the structural exactness expected of certified engineering and home renovation professionals.

### Token Applications
- **Base Components (`rounded`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Form input fields, dropdown trigger buttons, localized city selection chips, and table row groupings.
- **Cards & Containers (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Service overview blocks, pricing modules, technician verified bios, and quote breakdown summaries.
- **Hero Containers & Sticky Panels (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Section framing containers, media viewports, and interactive cost calculator surfaces.
- **Pill Exception (`rounded-full`, 9999px):** Exclusively designated for status badges ("Disponible Hoy", "Garantía 12 Meses", "Urgencias") and localized municipality micro-pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Action/Emergency):** Solid Craftsman Amber (`#d97706`) with white bold text (`#ffffff`), hover shift to `#b45309`. Subtle transition with `0 4px 12px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary (Direct Navy):** Deep slate fill (`#0f172a`) with white text. Used for "Ver Tarifas", "Descargar Factura", or technical documentation.
- **Tertiary (Ghost/Outline):** Transparent fill with `1px solid #e2e8f0`, text in `#1e293b`. On hover: border shifts to `#94a3b8` and background to `#f1f5f9`.
- **Sizing:** Standard touch-target height of 48px on mobile (`padding: 0 1.25rem`), scaling to 44px on desktop.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Crisp `#ffffff` container bounded by a `1px solid #e2e8f0` border and `rounded-lg` (16px) corners.
- Category headers feature an icon well: a 44x44px rounded box in `#f1f5f9` housing a high-precision monochrome slate or amber outline icon.
- Hover states initiate an upward 2px translate and border transition toward amber or deep slate.

### Chips & Location Badges
- **Municipal Tag:** Compact `#f1f5f9` pill (`rounded-full`) with `#475569` text and a subtle map marker icon. Indicating coverage in "Terrassa", "Sabadell", "Sant Cugat del Vallès", "Rubí", etc.
- **Status Indicator:** Pill container with a pulsing micro-dot (`#16a34a` for immediate availability, `#d97706` for peak queue).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #cbd5e1`, radius `0.5rem`. Height 46px.
- Focus state: Outline drops, replaced by a dual-ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px #ffffff, 0 0 0 4px #0f172a`.
- Floating labels in `#64748b` transitions to bold miniature tracking on active input.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base: 20x20px square with 4px border radius for checkboxes; round for radios.
- Unchecked: `1.5px solid #cbd5e1` with white interior.
- Checked: `#0f172a` primary fill with crisp white check mark or dot. Amber accent variant available for urgent checklist confirmation.

### Pricing Tables & Transparency Rows
- Transparent matrix featuring divided lines in `1px solid #f1f5f9`.
- "Tarifa Base por Hora" and "Desplazamiento" clearly separated into distinct visual callouts with large numerical data points set in bold display style (`display-hero-mobile` scale), avoiding hidden fee friction.

### Trustworthy Review & Certification Badges
- Grouped horizontal strip featuring an aggregate score card (e.g., "4.9/5 en Google Reviews"), combined with verified badge elements displaying ISO / electrical authorization credentials with `#16a34a` micro-badges and subtle star arrays in amber `#f59e0b`.
